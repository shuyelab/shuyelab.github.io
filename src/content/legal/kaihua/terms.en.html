2. Free features, Kaihua Pro and Remove Ads

The App contains the HSK 3.0 word lists for levels 1–6 (5,400 words), 1,940 characters for levels 1–6 and 244 grammar points for levels 1–3.

  • Free: the full 7-Day Tone Camp; all HSK 1 words, characters and grammar points; from HSK 2 onward, up to 10 new words a day (5 more for each optional rewarded video) and 5 new characters a day; search with word details and the audio that is available; the first 20 entries of the HSK 2.0 vs 3.0 change list. Reviews are always free and never limited. Free users may see ads, but never during a study session.
  • Kaihua Pro (paid): unlimited new words and characters at every level, browsing and previewing any level, grammar points for levels 2–3, exam prep (a daily plan toward your test date and mock tests in the official format for levels 1–3), the full HSK 2.0 vs 3.0 change list, memory curve details, a male voice in addition to the default female voice, and no ads.
  • Remove Ads (paid, offered in some regions): a one-time purchase that removes ads only. It does not include Pro features. With Remove Ads, the optional rewarded videos (extra new words, streak repair) are no longer offered.

Audio for levels 5–6 is not yet included; those words show “Audio coming soon” and listening exercises skip them until the audio is added in an update. We may improve or change features over time. If we remove a core paid feature, we will tell you in advance where reasonably possible.

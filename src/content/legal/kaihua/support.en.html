Frequently asked questions

I bought Pro or Remove Ads but it isn’t active / I have a new phone

Open Settings → Kaihua Pro and tap Restore purchases. This restores both Pro and Remove Ads. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account or Google account you used to buy; purchases cannot move between iPhone and Android. If it still doesn’t work, email us your store order ID (Apple: in the receipt email; Google: starts with “GPA.”).

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not inside the App:

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Kaihua. Apple help
  • Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Kaihua. Google help

On the App Store, cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge; on Google Play, cancel before the renewal date. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription. The lifetime purchase and Remove Ads are one-time payments and never renew.

How do I get a refund?

Payments go through Apple or Google, so refunds are handled by them: reportaproblem.apple.com (Apple) · Google Play refund help. For Google Play purchases you can also email us your order ID (it starts with “GPA.”) and we will look at the request.

What is free, and what do Pro and Remove Ads add?

Free: the whole 7-Day Tone Camp, everything in HSK 1, 10 new words a day from HSK 2 (plus 5 for each optional short video), 5 new characters a day from level 2, search with word details, and unlimited reviews.

Kaihua Pro (monthly, yearly or lifetime): unlimited new words and characters at every level, grammar for levels 2–3, exam prep with mock tests, the full HSK 2.0 vs 3.0 change list, memory curves, a male voice, and no ads.

Remove Ads (one-time, in some regions): removes ads only, without Pro features; the optional reward videos are no longer offered. Prices are shown in the App and the store, and vary by region.

Will my progress move to my new phone?

Not at the moment. Kaihua has no account, and your progress is stored only on your device. There is no cloud sync yet, so uninstalling the App or changing phones starts your progress from zero, unless your phone’s own backup restores it (your purchases can always be restored). We plan to add optional sync later and will update the Privacy Policy before it is released.

How do I delete my data?

Learning progress and settings: they live only on your device. Uninstall the App to delete them. Copies may remain in your phone’s own iCloud or Android backup; delete those in your device or account settings if you want them gone too.

Purchase records (RevenueCat): email shuyelab@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request” and your store order ID. No account or login is needed. Uninstall Kaihua first, because the App reconnects to RevenueCat each time it starts. We will ask RevenueCat to delete the record linked to the order and confirm within 15 business days (10 days for requests under Korea’s PIPA). Your purchase history with Apple or Google is kept by the store, so you can still restore your purchase later. If you never bought anything, RevenueCat holds only the random app user ID and technical data; the App does not show this ID, so we cannot match it to you, and uninstalling the App ends its use.

Ad data (Google): change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices (where shown); on iPhone, turn off tracking for Kaihua in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android, delete or reset your advertising ID in system settings. These choices affect future use; to ask Google about data it already holds, use the tools linked from the Google Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses this data.

Emails you sent us: ask us and we will delete them.

Why do I see ads? Can I change ad privacy settings?

Free users may see one ad on the “session complete” screen, and can choose to watch a short video for 5 extra new words or to repair a streak. No ads appear while you study, there are no pop-up ads, and Pro and Remove Ads have no ads.

  • Change ad consent: in regions where a privacy message is required (for example the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or some US states), open Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • iPhone tracking: if you allowed tracking and changed your mind, go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and switch Kaihua off (or turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”). Ads will then no longer use your advertising identifier.
  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by phone) to delete or reset your advertising ID.
Why does Kaihua ask how old I am?

To show the right kind of ads. You pick an age group once during setup; no birth date is asked for or stored, and the answer stays on your phone. Under 13 (under 14 when your device region is Korea or your phone language is Korean): no ads at all, and we suggest studying with a parent or guardian. 13–17 (14–17 in Korea): only non-personalised ads, and no tracking prompt on iPhone. Details are in section 5 of the Privacy Policy.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

In the App: Settings → Daily reminder. Kaihua only reminds you when words are about to fade, at the time you usually study or at a time you choose, and not after you’ve finished for the day. It asks for notification permission after your first completed session, or when you turn reminders on. You can also turn off notifications for Kaihua in your phone’s system settings. Reminders are worked out on your device; no data is sent anywhere.

How do I add the home screen widget?

Android: long-press your home screen → Widgets → Kaihua (2×2 or 4×2). The widget shows a word of the day and how many words are due, using data stored on your phone. An iPhone widget is planned.

Which HSK version and levels does Kaihua cover?

HSK 3.0, based on the official syllabus released in November 2025: all 5,400 words of levels 1–6, 1,940 characters, and 244 grammar points for levels 1–3. Audio for levels 1–4 is built in; audio for levels 5–6 is coming in an update, and until then those words show “Audio coming soon”. Mock tests in the official format cover levels 1–3. Words that are new or moved levels compared with HSK 2.0 are marked. HSK is a registered trademark of CLEC; Kaihua is not affiliated with CLEC or Chinese Testing International.

I found a mistake in a word, example or audio

Thank you! Email us the word (Chinese characters), your explanation language and what looks wrong; a screenshot helps. Memory hooks are mnemonics, not etymology. How the content is made is explained in section 7 of the Privacy Policy.

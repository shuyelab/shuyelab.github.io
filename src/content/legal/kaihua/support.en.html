Frequently asked questions

I bought Pro but it isn’t unlocked / I have a new phone

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy. If it still doesn’t work, email us your store order ID (Apple: in the receipt email; Google: starts with “GPA.”).

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not inside the App:

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Kaihua. Apple help
  • Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Kaihua. Google help

Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription. The lifetime purchase never renews.

How do I get a refund?

Payments go through Apple or Google, so refunds are handled by them: reportaproblem.apple.com (Apple) · Google Play refund help.

Will my progress move to my new phone?

Not at the moment. Kaihua has no account, and your progress is stored only on your device. There is no cloud sync yet, so uninstalling the App or changing phones starts your progress from zero (your Pro purchase can still be restored). We plan to add optional sync later and will update the Privacy Policy before it ships.

How do I delete my data?

Learning progress and settings: they live only on your device. Uninstall the App to delete them completely.

Purchase records (RevenueCat): email shuyelab@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request” and your store order ID. We will ask RevenueCat to delete the anonymous record linked to it and confirm within 15 business days. Your purchase history with Apple or Google itself is kept by the store.

Ad data (Google): change your choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices (where shown); on iPhone, turn off tracking for Kaihua in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in system settings. See how Google uses this data.

Emails you sent us: ask us and we will delete them.

Why do I see ads? Can I change ad privacy settings?

Free users may see one ad on the “session complete” screen, and can choose to watch a short video for 5 extra new words or to repair a streak. No ads appear while you study, there are no pop-up interstitials, and Pro has no ads.

  • Change ad consent: in regions where a privacy message is required (for example the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or some US states), open Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • iPhone tracking: if you allowed tracking and changed your mind, go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and switch Kaihua off (or turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”). Ads will then no longer use your advertising identifier.
  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by phone) to reset or delete your advertising ID.
How do I turn reminders on or off?

In the App: Settings → Daily reminder. Kaihua only reminds you when words are about to fade, at the time you usually study, and not after you’ve finished for the day. It asks for notification permission after your first completed session. You can also turn off notifications for Kaihua in your phone’s system settings. Reminders are worked out on your device; no data is sent anywhere.

How do I add the home screen widget?

Android: long-press your home screen → Widgets → Kaihua (2×2 or 4×2). The widget shows a word of the day and how many words are due, using data stored on your phone. An iPhone widget is planned.

I found a mistake in a word, example or audio

Thank you! Some audio and text in Kaihua is generated with AI and reviewed by people, so mistakes can slip through. Email us the word (Chinese characters), your explanation language and what looks wrong; a screenshot helps. Memory hooks are mnemonics, not etymology.

Which HSK version does Kaihua use?

HSK 3.0 levels 1–3 (1,000 words), based on the official syllabus released in November 2025. Words that are new or moved levels compared with HSK 2.0 are marked. HSK is a registered trademark of CLEC; Kaihua is not affiliated with CLEC or Chinese Testing International.

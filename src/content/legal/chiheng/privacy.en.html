Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026 · Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains how the ChiHeng app (持恒) for Android, iPhone / iPad and HarmonyOS (“ChiHeng”) handles your information. ChiHeng is developed by Shuye Lab (书叶工作室) and is a free, non-commercial tool for keeping a record of your daily practice. If you have any question, write to shuyelab@gmail.com.

The short version
  • ChiHeng is currently in testing and is not yet available in any app store. If anything changes before the public release, we will update this page.
  • No sign-up and no sign-in. There is no account.
  • We do not collect your personal information, and we have no server that receives your data.
  • Your practices, records, notes and library stay on your device. There is no cloud sync.
  • No ads, no membership or paid features, no analytics, no crash reporting and no other third-party SDKs.
  • You export backups yourself and decide where to keep them.
  • Uninstalling ChiHeng deletes the data inside the app (backup files you saved elsewhere are not affected).

1. We do not collect personal information

ChiHeng never asks for your name, phone number, email address, contacts or location, and it does not read device identifiers or advertising identifiers. What you record in ChiHeng is not sent to us or to anyone else.

The only exception is when you choose to email us, see section 8.

2. Network access and third-party components

Everything ChiHeng does happens on your device. It does not need an internet connection:

  • Android: the app does not request the internet permission (INTERNET), and Android does not let an app without it access the network. The manifest does include “view network state” (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE). This is declared by the Media3 playback library; it can only read whether the device is online and cannot connect to anything. ChiHeng uses Media3 only to play files on your device.
  • HarmonyOS: the app does not request the internet permission (ohos.permission.INTERNET) and has no third-party packages.
  • iPhone / iPad: iOS has no separate internet permission. ChiHeng’s code makes no network requests, and apart from Apple’s system frameworks it uses no third-party libraries.

If a book or PDF you are reading contains a web link and you tap it, the link opens in your system browser. What happens after that is handled by the browser, not by ChiHeng.

3. Where your data is kept

The following is stored in ChiHeng’s private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read:

  • practices and check-ins (including days you filled in later and automatic check-ins), and any totals you entered from before you started using ChiHeng;
  • notes (text, tags, links), and images you insert into notes — a copy of each image is kept in the app’s private folder;
  • your library’s shelf, collections, sections, progress, bookmarks and highlights;
  • app settings, such as the day cutoff time, bells and reading preferences.

Library files (PDF, EPUB, TXT, audio, video, image folders) are handled differently on each platform:

  • Android: you pick files or folders with the system file picker, and ChiHeng only gets permission to read them. The files stay where they are and are not copied or changed. Files opened or shared into ChiHeng from other apps, and packages shared by Dharma friends, are copied into ChiHeng.
  • iPhone / iPad and HarmonyOS: imported files are copied into ChiHeng. The originals are left untouched.

On Android, ChiHeng turns off the system’s app data backup (allowBackup), so its data is not handed to the system’s cloud backup.

4. Backups, sharing and export

In Me › Data › Export backup, ChiHeng creates a backup file and passes it to the system’s save or share screen. You decide where it goes, for example your phone, a computer or a cloud drive. Once the file leaves ChiHeng, it is managed by wherever you saved it; we have no access to it.

  • On Android, the backup is a .json file with your practices, records, prior practice, note text, and your library’s structure, progress, bookmarks and highlights. It does not include the library files themselves, images in notes, or app settings.
  • On iPhone / iPad and HarmonyOS, the backup is a single file that also contains the library files stored in ChiHeng and the images in your notes.

When you share an item or a collection from ChiHeng, the system share sheet opens and the content goes only where you send it. If you choose to include your highlights and notes, they are added to the shared package.

5. System permissions

ChiHeng only needs a permission when you use the feature that relies on it, and you can turn any of them off in system settings. ChiHeng does not use the camera, microphone, location or contacts, and it does not ask for access to your whole photo library or all of your storage.

6. Deleting your data

  • Records, practices, notes and library items can each be deleted inside the app. Library files stored in ChiHeng can be cleared in Me › Library storage.
  • Uninstalling ChiHeng deletes all data inside the app, including the database, note images and copied library files.
  • Backup files you exported, and on Android the original files in your own folders, are not in ChiHeng’s storage. Uninstalling does not delete them; remove them yourself if you want to.
  • We keep none of your usage data, so there is nothing to ask us to delete, apart from any emails you sent us (see section 8).

7. Children

ChiHeng does not collect personal information from anyone, so it does not collect personal information from children. Minors should use ChiHeng with the knowledge of a parent or guardian.

8. When you email us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever your message contains, including attachments and screenshots. We use it only to reply to you and to look into the problem you report. You can ask us to delete our correspondence at any time.

9. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we will post the update on this page and change the “Last updated” date. If a change affects how data is handled (for example, a feature that goes online or a new third-party component), we will tell you in the app or in the release notes before it takes effect.

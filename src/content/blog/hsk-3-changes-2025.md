The Chinese Proficiency Test (HSK, 汉语水平考试) underwent its biggest change in over a decade when HSK 3.0 officially launched in 2025. If you're learning Chinese or preparing for the exam, here's what you need to know about the new structure.

## What Changed in HSK 3.0?

### 1. New Level Structure

The old HSK 2.0 had six levels (HSK 1–6). HSK 3.0 reorganized this into **three bands with three levels each**, plus beginner, intermediate, and advanced bands:

- **Beginner Band**: Levels 1–3 (replaces old HSK 1–2)
- **Intermediate Band**: Levels 4–6 (replaces old HSK 3–4)
- **Advanced Band**: Levels 7–9 (replaces old HSK 5–6)

### 2. Updated Word Lists

The official syllabus now includes **11,092 words** across all nine levels, up from 5,000 in HSK 2.0. This means:

- **Level 1**: 500 words (old HSK 1 had 150)
- **Level 2**: 772 words (old HSK 2 had 300)
- **Level 3**: 973 words (old HSK 3 had 600)
- **Level 4**: 1,000 words
- **Level 5**: 1,071 words
- **Level 6**: 1,140 words

Each level builds on the previous ones, so Level 4 expects you to know all 2,245 words from Levels 1–3.

### 3. Reading and Writing Split

HSK 3.0 distinguishes between **reading-only characters** (认读字) and **writing characters** (书写字). For example:

- **Level 1**: You need to recognize 300 characters but only write 100 of them
- **Level 2**: Recognize 448 characters, write 125
- **Level 3**: Recognize 590 characters, write 150

This reflects real-world usage: most learners can read more characters than they can write from memory.

### 4. New Question Types

The test format added new task-based questions that simulate real communication scenarios:

- **Short writing tasks** (write a WeChat message, fill out a form)
- **Integrated listening and reading** (listen to a conversation, then answer questions about a related text)
- **Practical scenarios** (booking appointments, asking for directions)

## How to Prepare for HSK 3.0

### 1. Use the Official 2025 Syllabus

Make sure your study materials follow the **2025 syllabus** (released November 2024). Older materials based on HSK 2.0 won't cover the new word lists or question formats.

### 2. Practice Stroke Order

Since you'll need to write characters by hand (not just recognize them), review stroke order for the writing characters at your level. Tools like our [Grid by Grid Chinese workbooks](/books/grid-by-grid-chinese/) provide stroke-by-stroke practice with pinyin and example sentences.

### 3. Focus on Tones

HSK 3.0 places more emphasis on listening comprehension. Use apps like [Kaihua](/apps/kaihua/) to train your ear to distinguish the four tones through pitch curve visualization.

### 4. Take Practice Tests

Official sample tests are available on the [CLTC website](https://www.chinesetest.cn/). Take at least two full practice tests before your exam date to get familiar with the timing and format.

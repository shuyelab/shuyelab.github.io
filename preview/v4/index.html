About

A small shelf of useful things.

Shuye Lab is an independent studio. We make apps for learning Chinese and tracking daily practice, and print books for study: Chinese characters, the U.S. citizenship test, classical Chinese texts in translation, chess tactics.

  • i.
    Official sources firstWord lists come from the HSK 3.0 syllabus; civics questions and answers from the USCIS list.
  • ii.
    Your progress stays with youOur apps keep everything on your device and work without an account.
  • iii.
    Free things stay freeKaihua reviews, all of Chiheng, and the citizenship audio. No login for any of them.

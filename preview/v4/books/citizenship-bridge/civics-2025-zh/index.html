Who it is for

Study for the 2025 civics test in Chinese and English, in large print.

For Chinese-speaking applicants who file Form N-400 on or after October 20, 2025, and the family members who help them study.

专为母语是中文、2025 年 10 月 20 日及以后递交 N-400 的申请人编写。英文题目与答案逐字来自 USCIS 公布的题单，每一条官方答案都有中文。

What is inside

One card per question.

128

Only the 2025 test

All 128 civics questions. English questions and answers word for word from the USCIS list (M-1778, 09/25).

Aa

Large print

8.5 × 11 in pages. English questions 19 pt, answers 18 pt, Chinese 18 pt, notes 16 pt.

中

Chinese for every answer

Every official answer has Chinese, matched line by line.

Q1–128

A card for each question

A recommended answer chosen from the official answers, stress marks, background notes, a one-line memory aid, and pointers to look-alike questions.

65/20

The 65/20 chapter

For applicants 65 or older with 20+ years as permanent residents: the 20 starred questions, 22 pt Chinese, four practice tests and 20 cut-out fold cards.

As of

Answers that change

President, Vice President, Speaker and Chief Justice are marked with a date. For your own senators, representative, governor and capital: lookup steps and a “My answers” card.

60

The English test

Common N-400 interview phrasings, the official reading and writing vocabulary, and 60 practice sentences written only with it.

7 × 20

Practice tests

Seven 20-question tests that together cover all 128. State capitals table and a one-page index of all questions.

What's inside

Inside Kaihua. Real screens.

Every screen on this page is the app itself, with the word list that ships in it.

01 · Tone Camp

Seven days to hear the four tones.

See each tone as a pitch curve, hear it, then draw the pitch you heard with your finger.

  • Minimal pairs and all 20 two-syllable tone combinations
  • Tone changes for third tones, 一 yī and 不 bù
  • A graduation test at the end, then a short daily warm-up
02 · Word cards

Answer first, then see the card.

Kaihua grades you from your answer and how fast you gave it. Tap “Too easy” when a word is already yours.

  • Collocations and two example sentences with audio
  • Every word tagged against HSK 2.0: new, moved, or the same
  • Pinyin fades as you master a word
03 · Search

Look up anything, even without tones.

Search words, characters and grammar points by characters, by pinyin with or without tone marks, or by meaning.

  • Same sound, different tones: 买 mǎi and 卖 mài side by side
  • Words beyond your level show their HSK level
04 · Characters

Built from parts.

Strokes, radical and components for every character: 爱 = 爫 + 冖 + 友. Memory hooks are labeled “mnemonic, not etymology”.

05 · Grammar & mock tests

Practice in the exam's own formats.

Short grammar quizzes with explanations, and mock tests that follow the official HSK question types (Pro).

Memory Garden

Review right before you forget.

Reviews are planned by FSRS, an open-source memory model, and fit the study time you choose each day. Every word is a plant in your Memory Garden: review on time and it blooms, wait too long and it wilts.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Which HSK does Kaihua cover?

HSK 3.0, the syllabus published in November 2025. The first release covers levels 1–3, 1,000 words in all. Every word is tagged against HSK 2.0, so you can see what is new and what moved.

Does Kaihua score my pronunciation?

No. Tone training is listening-first: you hear, compare and draw the pitch. Speech scoring is often wrong, so we left it out.

Is it free?

The Tone Camp, all of HSK 1 and every review are free. Pro adds unlimited new words, all levels, exam pacing and no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Progress is stored on your phone and the app works offline.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Not yet. There is no cloud sync at the moment. A Pro purchase can be restored on a new phone with Restore purchases.

Where does the word list come from?

Straight from the official HSK 3.0 syllabus. Definitions and examples are checked against reference dictionaries.

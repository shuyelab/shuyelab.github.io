What's inside

Everything for the civics test, in two languages.

Study for the 2025 U.S. citizenship civics test in Chinese and English, in large print.

  • 01
    Only the 2025 testAll 128 civics questions. English questions and answers word for word from the USCIS list (M-1778, 09/25).
  • 02
    Chinese for every official answerMatched line by line, for applicants who study or take the test with Chinese.
  • 03
    One card per questionA recommended answer chosen only from the official answers, stress marks, background notes, a one-line memory aid, and pointers to look-alike questions.
  • 04
    A 65/20 chapterFor applicants 65 or older with 20+ years as permanent residents: the 20 starred questions, simplified with traditional Chinese, four 10-question tests, 20 cut-out fold cards.
  • 05
    Answers that changePresident, Vice President, Speaker and Chief Justice carry an as-of date. For your own senators, representative, governor and capital: lookup steps and a “My answers” card.
  • 06
    The English test tooCommon N-400 interview phrasings, the official reading and writing vocabulary, and 60 practice sentences written only with it.
  • 07
    Seven practice testsTwenty questions each, covering all 128 between them.
  • 08
    Quick referencesA table of state capitals and a one-page index of all 128 questions.

A chapter for older applicants

If you are 65 or older and have been a permanent resident for 20 years or more, you study only 20 starred questions and may take the test in Chinese. The book gathers them in one place, in larger type, with four short practice tests and cut-out cards.

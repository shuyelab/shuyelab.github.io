What's inside

The word list comes from the official syllabus published in November 2025. Every word is tagged against HSK 2.0: new in 3.0, moved from another level, or the same as before.

Free for everyone

7-Day Tone Camp

See each tone as a pitch curve, hear it, then draw the pitch you heard with your finger. Minimal pairs, all 20 two-syllable tone combinations, and the tone changes for third tones, 一 and 不.

Listening first. There is no speech scoring, because it is so often wrong.

Answer first, then the card

Graded automatically from your answer and how fast you gave it. Collocations, two example sentences with audio and character components on every card. Pinyin fades as you master a word.

Reviews planned by FSRS

FSRS is an open-source memory model. It plans each review around the study time you choose. Every word is a plant in your Memory Garden: review on time and it blooms, wait too long and it wilts.

Reviews are always free and never capped.

Search everything

Words, characters and grammar points, by characters, pinyin with or without tones, or meaning. Same sound, different tones: 买 mǎi and 卖 mài side by side.

Characters, piece by piece

Strokes, radical and components, like 爱 = 爫 + 冖 + 友. Memory hooks are labeled "mnemonic, not etymology".

Grammar and mock tests

Short grammar quizzes with explanations, and mock tests in the official HSK question formats.

Which HSK does Kaihua cover?

HSK 3.0, the syllabus published in November 2025. The first release covers levels 1–3, which is 1,000 words. Every word is tagged against HSK 2.0.

Does Kaihua score my pronunciation?

No. Tone training is listening-first: you hear, compare and draw the pitch. Speech scoring is often wrong, so we left it out.

Is it free?

The Tone Camp, all of HSK 1 and every review are free. Pro adds unlimited new words, all levels, exam pacing and no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Progress is stored on your phone and the app works offline.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Not yet. There is no cloud sync at the moment. A Pro purchase can be restored on a new phone.

Where does the word list come from?

Straight from the official HSK 3.0 syllabus. Definitions and examples are checked against reference dictionaries.

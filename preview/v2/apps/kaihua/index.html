Chapter one

7-Day Tone Camp

See each tone as a pitch curve, hear it, then draw the pitch you heard with your finger. Over seven days you work through minimal pairs, all 20 two-syllable tone combinations, and the tone changes for third tones, 一 and 不.

Listening first. There is no speech scoring, because it is so often wrong.

Chapter two

Answer first, then the card

Each card asks before it tells. Grading is automatic, from your answer and how fast you gave it. Every card has collocations, two example sentences with audio, and the components of each character. Pinyin fades as you master a word.

Reviews are planned by FSRS, an open-source memory model, and adapt to the study time you choose. Every word is a plant in your Memory Garden: review on time and it blooms, wait too long and it wilts.

Reviews are always free and never capped.

Chapter three

Search everything

Look up words, characters and grammar points by characters, by pinyin with or without tones, or by meaning. When one spelling has several tones, a card puts them side by side: 买 mǎi, to buy, and 卖 mài, to sell.

Characters

Piece by piece

Strokes, radical and components (爱 = 爫 + 冖 + 友), with memory hooks labeled “mnemonic, not etymology”.

Grammar

Short grammar quizzes

One grammar point at a time, with an explanation after every answer.

Questions

Frequently asked

Which HSK does Kaihua cover?

HSK 3.0, the syllabus published in November 2025. The first release covers levels 1–3, 1,000 words. Every word is tagged against HSK 2.0: new in HSK 3.0, moved from another level, or the same as before.

Does Kaihua score my pronunciation?

No. Tone training is listening-first: you hear, compare and draw the pitch. Speech scoring is often wrong, so we left it out.

Is it free?

The Tone Camp, all of HSK 1 and every review are free. Pro adds unlimited new words, all levels, exam pacing and no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Progress is stored on your phone, and the words and audio are inside the app, so it works offline.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Not yet. There is no cloud sync at the moment. A Pro purchase can be restored on a new phone.

Where does the word list come from?

Straight from the official HSK 3.0 syllabus. Definitions and examples are checked against reference dictionaries.

How do I cancel Pro?

Subscriptions are managed in your App Store or Google Play account. The lifetime purchase never renews.

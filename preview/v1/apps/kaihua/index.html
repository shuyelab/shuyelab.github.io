Features

Built on the November 2025 syllabus.

The word list comes straight from the official HSK 3.0 syllabus. Every word is tagged against HSK 2.0: new, moved, or the same as before.

7-Day Tone Camp

See the pitch, then draw what you hear.

Each tone appears as a pitch curve. Listen, then trace the pitch you heard with your finger.

  • Minimal pairs and all 20 two-syllable tone combinations
  • Tone changes for third tones, 一 and 不
  • Listening first. No unreliable speech scoring.
Word cards

Answer first, then see the card.

Grading is automatic, based on your answer and how fast you gave it. Tap “Too easy” when a word is already yours.

  • Collocations and two example sentences with audio
  • Character components on every card
  • Pinyin fades as you master a word
Search

Type mai, see both.

Search words, characters and grammar points by characters, pinyin with or without tones, or meaning. When one spelling hides several words, a card puts them side by side.

  • 买 mǎi, to buy · 卖 mài, to sell
  • Meanings in English, Korean and Thai
Characters, grammar, mock tests

Characters piece by piece.

Strokes, radical and components for each character: 爱 = 爫 + 冖 + 友. Memory hooks are labeled “mnemonic, not etymology”.

  • Short grammar quizzes with explanations
  • Mock tests in the official HSK question formats (Pro)
FSRS spaced repetition

Review right before you forget.

Reviews are planned by FSRS, an open-source memory model, and adapt to the study time you choose each day. Every word is a plant in your Memory Garden: review on time and it blooms, wait too long and it wilts.

FAQ

Questions

Which HSK does Kaihua cover?

HSK 3.0, the syllabus published in November 2025. The first release covers levels 1–3, 1,000 words. Every word is tagged against HSK 2.0.

Does Kaihua score my pronunciation?

No. Tone training is listening-first: you hear, compare and draw the pitch. Speech scoring is often wrong, so we left it out.

Is it free?

The Tone Camp, all of HSK 1 and every review are free. Pro adds unlimited new words, all levels, exam pacing and no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Progress is stored on your phone and the app works offline.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Not yet. There is no cloud sync at the moment. A Pro purchase can be restored on a new phone.

Where does the word list come from?

Straight from the official HSK 3.0 syllabus. Definitions and examples are checked against reference dictionaries.

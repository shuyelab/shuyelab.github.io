Who it’s for

For the applicant, and the family member who helps.

Study for the 2025 U.S. citizenship civics test in Chinese and English, in large print.

Written for Chinese-speaking applicants who file Form N-400 on or after October 20, 2025, and for the family members who help them study.

专为母语是中文、2025 年 10 月 20 日及以后递交 N-400 的申请人编写。

Inside the book

Every question, on its own card.

English questions and answers come word for word from the USCIS list (M-1778, 09/25).

128

Only the 2025 test

All 128 civics questions of the 2025 version, and nothing from older lists.

19pt

Large print throughout

8.5 × 11 in pages. English questions 19 pt, answers 18 pt; Chinese 18 pt; notes 16 pt. The 65/20 pages use 22 pt Chinese.

中/EN

Chinese for every answer

Each official answer has its Chinese, matched line by line.

1card per question

A recommended answer, and why

An answer chosen only from the official ones, stress marks for pronunciation, background notes, a one-line memory aid, and pointers to questions that are easy to mix up.

65/20

A chapter for seniors

The 20 starred questions together, simplified Chinese with traditional, four 10-question practice tests and 20 cut-out fold cards.

7

Practice tests

Seven 20-question tests that together cover all 128, plus a state capitals table and a one-page index.

60

Reading and writing practice

Common N-400 interview phrasings, the official reading and writing vocabulary, and 60 practice sentences written only with that vocabulary.

as of

Answers that change are dated

President, Vice President, Speaker and Chief Justice carry an as-of date. For your own senators, representative, governor and capital: step-by-step lookup and a “My answers” card.

Free companion audio

Hear every question before the interview.

Every English question and recommended answer, read at normal and slow speed. The 20 questions for 65/20 are also read in Chinese. 20 units that match the book. No login.

免费朗读音频：常速、慢速两种，65/20 二十题另有中文问答，不用登录。

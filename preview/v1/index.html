About

书 shū means book. 叶 yè means leaf.

Shuye Lab is an independent studio. We make apps for learning Chinese and tracking daily practice, and print books for study: Chinese characters, the U.S. citizenship test, classical Chinese texts in translation, and chess tactics.

The books are the 书. The 叶 is the part that grows a little every day you come back.

01

Official sources first

Kaihua's word list is the HSK 3.0 syllabus. The citizenship book uses the USCIS civics list, word for word.

02

No account, your device

Our apps work without signing up. Progress stays on your phone.

03

Free things stay free

Reviews in Kaihua are never capped. Chiheng has no paid features. The citizenship audio needs no login.

Apps

Two apps for two kinds of daily habit: learning words, and keeping a practice going. No account in either one.

For learners of Chinese

Kaihua 开花 kāihuā

Learn the words of the new HSK 3.0 exam and finally hear the tones. The name means “to bloom”.

1,000
words, HSK 3.0 levels 1–3, from the official syllabus published November 2025
7 days
Tone Camp: see the pitch, hear it, draw it with your finger
Free
reviews, always and without a cap
3
interface languages: English, 한국어, ไทย
About Kaihua iPhone & Android coming soon
A quieter tool

Chiheng持恒

A free practice log for daily recitation, meditation and reading.

  • A counter with bead mode, 108 per round
  • Daily check-ins and a calendar
  • A meditation timer with bells
  • A library for your own files

Free for good: no ads, no membership, no paid features. No login, records stay on your phone, and a full backup can be exported at any time. Chinese interface. Android, iPhone & iPad, HarmonyOS, in testing.

About

书叶

书 shū means book. 叶 yè means leaf.

Shuye Lab is an independent studio. We make apps for learning Chinese and for keeping a daily practice, and we print books for study: Chinese characters, the U.S. citizenship test, classical Chinese texts in translation, chess tactics.

  1. Official sources firstWord lists and answers come from the source: the HSK 3.0 syllabus, the USCIS civics list.
  2. Your data stays with youNo account needed in our apps. Progress stays on your device.
  3. Free things stay freeKaihua reviews, all of Chiheng, and the citizenship audio. No login for any of them.

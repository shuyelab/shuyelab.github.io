7-Day Tone Camp

See the tone, hear it, then draw it.

Each tone appears as a pitch curve. Listen, then trace the pitch you heard with your finger.

  • Minimal pairs and all 20 two-syllable tone combinations
  • Tone changes for third tones, 一 and 不
  • Listening first. No speech scoring, because it is so often wrong
Word cards

Answer first, then see the card.

Kaihua grades you from your answer and how fast you gave it. Tap “Too easy” when a word is already yours.

  • Collocations and two example sentences with audio
  • Character components on every card
  • Pinyin fades as you master a word, so you learn to read the characters

FAQ

Questions

Which HSK does Kaihua cover?

HSK 3.0, the syllabus published in November 2025. The first release covers levels 1–3, 1,000 words. Every word is tagged against HSK 2.0.

Does Kaihua score my pronunciation?

No. Tone training is listening-first: you hear, compare and draw the pitch. Speech scoring is often wrong, so we left it out.

Is it free?

The Tone Camp, all of HSK 1 and every review are free. Pro adds unlimited new words, all levels, exam pacing and no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Progress is stored on your phone and the app works offline.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Not yet. There is no cloud sync at the moment. A Pro purchase can be restored on a new phone.

Where does the word list come from?

Straight from the official HSK 3.0 syllabus. Definitions and examples are checked against reference dictionaries.

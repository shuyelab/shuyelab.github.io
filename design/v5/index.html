03About

Shuye Lab is an independent studio. We make apps for learning Chinese and tracking daily practice, and print books for study.

Where the name comes from
书shū · book
叶yè · leaf

Books are what we print. A leaf is something that grows a little every day, which is how most studying works.

Official sources first

Word lists come from the HSK 3.0 syllabus, and civics answers come word for word from the USCIS list.

No account needed

Our apps keep your progress on your device. There is nothing to sign up for.

Free things stay free

Reviews in Kaihua are always free. Chiheng has no ads and nothing to buy. The citizenship audio needs no login.

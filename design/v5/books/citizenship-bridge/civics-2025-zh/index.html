Who it is for

Chinese-speaking applicants who file Form N-400 on or after October 20, 2025, and the family members who help them study.

专为母语是中文、2025 年 10 月 20 日及以后递交 N-400 的申请人编写。

What's inside

Only the 2025 test. English questions and answers are word for word from the USCIS list (M-1778, 09/25), and every official answer has Chinese beside it, line by line.

1

One card per question

A recommended answer chosen only from the official answers, stress marks for pronunciation, background notes, a one-line memory aid, and pointers to questions that are easy to mix up.

128civics questions
7practice tests of 20
60writing practice sentences
2

The 65/20 chapter

For applicants 65 or older with 20+ years as permanent residents: the 20 starred questions together, simplified Chinese with traditional, four 10-question practice tests and 20 cut-out fold cards.

3

Answers that change

President, Vice President, Speaker and Chief Justice are marked with an as-of date. For your own senators, representative, governor and capital: step-by-step lookup and a "My answers" card.

4

The English test

Common N-400 interview phrasings, the official reading and writing vocabulary, and 60 practice sentences written only with that vocabulary.

5

Practice and look-up

Seven 20-question practice tests that together cover all 128, a table of state capitals, and a one-page index of all 128 questions.

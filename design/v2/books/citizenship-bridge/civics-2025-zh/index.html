Inside the book

What you will find, page by page

Only the 2025 test

All 128 civics questions. English questions and answers word for word from the USCIS list (M-1778, 09/25).

Large print

8.5 × 11 in pages. English questions in 19 pt, answers in 18 pt, Chinese in 18 pt, notes in 16 pt. The 65/20 pages use 22 pt Chinese.

Chinese for every answer

Every official answer has its Chinese, matched line by line, for applicants who study or test with an interpreter.

One card per question

A recommended answer chosen only from the official answers, stress marks for pronunciation, background notes, a one-line memory aid, and pointers to questions that are easy to mix up.

The 65/20 chapter

For applicants 65 or older with 20 or more years as permanent residents: the 20 starred questions together, simplified Chinese with traditional, four 10-question practice tests and 20 cut-out fold cards.

Answers that change

President, Vice President, Speaker and Chief Justice are marked with an as-of date. For your own senators, representative, governor and capital: step-by-step lookup and a “My answers” card.

The English test

Common N‑400 interview phrasings, the official reading and writing vocabulary, and 60 practice sentences written only with that vocabulary.

Seven practice tests

Seven 20-question tests that together cover all 128, a state capitals table and a one-page index of all 128 questions.

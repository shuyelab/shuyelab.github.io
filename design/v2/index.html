书叶

Shuye is two Chinese words. 书 shū means book, for the books we print. 叶 yè means leaf, for things that grow a little each day.

III · About

About the studio

Shuye Lab is an independent studio. We make apps for learning Chinese and for keeping a daily practice, and we print books for study: Chinese characters, the U.S. citizenship test, classical Chinese texts in translation, and chess tactics.

The subjects are different, but the readers have something in common. They study a little at a time, often on their own, and they want material they can trust.

  1. Lists come from the source.Kaihua’s word list is the official HSK 3.0 syllabus. The citizenship book uses the USCIS civics questions and answers word for word.
  2. No account needed.Our apps keep your progress on your own device.
  3. Free things stay free.Kaihua reviews are never capped, Chiheng has no paid features, and the citizenship audio needs no login.
